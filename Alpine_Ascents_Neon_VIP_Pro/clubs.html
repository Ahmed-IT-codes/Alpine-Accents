ALPINE CLIMBING

Mountain clubs and community

Clubs can help people learn outdoor skills, meet partners and find instruction. Before joining, check the organization's reputation, instructor qualifications, insurance arrangements and current activity calendar.

For young or new participants, choose supervised activities appropriate to their experience and follow the guidance of qualified adults and instructors.

How to evaluate a club

Training and safety

Look for clear training standards, safety briefings, appropriate supervision and transparent emergency plans.

Responsible access

Check whether the group follows local access rules, respects communities and minimizes environmental impact.

Find your community

Questions to ask a mountain club

A good community welcomes questions, explains expectations clearly and prioritises safe, inclusive participation.

Training and leaders

Ask about leader qualifications, beginner sessions, first aid knowledge, supervision and how routes are selected.

Trip organisation

Check group size, transport arrangements, equipment lists, costs, cancellation policies and emergency procedures.

Community values

Look for respect, environmental responsibility, clear communication and a culture where anyone can raise a safety concern.

Explore responsibly

Before visiting a mountain region

Research locally

  • Check access rules, permits and seasonal closures.
  • Ask about local weather and transport conditions.
  • Learn about cultural expectations and protected areas.
  • Use official sources for current travel advice.

Care for the outdoors

  • Pack out rubbish and avoid damaging plants.
  • Keep a respectful distance from wildlife.
  • Use established paths and campsites where required.
  • Support local guides and businesses responsibly.
