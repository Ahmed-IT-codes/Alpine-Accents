Understand the details

What to check before choosing a package

These sample prices are for a student-project demonstration, not live offers. Confirm all costs, inclusions and operator credentials before paying for any real trip.

Included services

Ask whether guides, accommodation, meals, transport, permits, equipment and emergency support are included or charged separately.

Difficulty and pace

Check daily distance, elevation gain, terrain, expected weather and the experience level required for every participant.

Cancellation terms

Review deposit rules, refund deadlines, weather-related changes, insurance requirements and the plan for itinerary changes.

Budget wisely

Common extra expenses

Before departure

  • Travel to the meeting point.
  • Personal clothing and footwear.
  • Travel insurance where appropriate.
  • Training, permits or required medical advice.

During the trip

  • Optional porterage or equipment rental.
  • Snacks, drinks and personal purchases.
  • Extra accommodation due to delays.
  • Contingency funds for unexpected changes.
