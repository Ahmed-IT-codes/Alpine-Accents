How to plan a mountain trip.

Great trips start with honest preparation—not with rushing toward the summit.

STEP 01

Learn

Understand route grades, terrain, seasonal conditions and what the trip demands.

STEP 02

Prepare

Build fitness gradually, practise navigation and prepare suitable equipment.

STEP 03

Plan

Check forecasts, permits, transport, emergency options and a realistic schedule.

STEP 04

Respect

Work as a team, follow local guidance and leave the outdoors clean.
